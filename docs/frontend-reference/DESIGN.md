## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for clinical psychologists and mental health practitioners navigating high-cognitive-load days filled with therapy sessions, clinical recordkeeping, supervisory tasks, and scheduling. It balances clinical rigor with emotional decompression.

### Personality & Demeanor
- **Composed & Therapeutic:** Unhurried, serene, and devoid of cognitive friction. The interface serves as an orderly sanctuary rather than a chaotic productivity tracker.
- **Ethical & Credible:** Grounded in clinical precision, confidentiality, and data hygiene, projecting professional reassurance at every level.
- **Ergonomic & Attentive:** Built to minimize repetitive strain during back-to-back patient notes and swift context-switching between therapy modalities.

### Design Movement: Modern Clinical Minimalism with Soft Tactile Elevation
The aesthetic marries clean Scandinavian functionalism with soft ambient depths. It relies on clean, high-contrast typography, generous cool slate-gray canvases, crisp structural cards, contextual color dots, and subtle borders instead of stark divider lines.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from clinical clarity: deep authoritative blues anchoring navigation and primary actions, balanced with soft slate backgrounds and warm amber/ochre accents for supervision, reflection, and milestones.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#005696`):** Deep cobalt blue. Powers primary CTAs, active navigation tabs, high-importance triggers, and focal brand headers.
- **Secondary (`#C99738`):** Muted amber ocre. Assigned to clinical supervision highlights, pending reviews, warm badges, and patient milestone alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#0F766E`):** Sea pine / deep emerald. Signifies completed sessions, confidential clearance states, and therapeutic progression.
- **Neutral Canvas & Tiers (`#F1F5F9`, `#E2E8F0`, `#0F172A`):**
  - App Canvas Surface: `#F1F5F9` (slate 100) — mitigates eye fatigue across 8-hour clinic days.
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (pure white) with `#E2E8F0` border strokes.
  - Text Primary: `#0F172A` (deep ink slate).
  - Text Muted: `#64748B` (slate 500).

### Semantic & Context Tags
- **Context Dots & Badges:**
  - *Atendimento / Pacientes:* Emerald `#059669`
  - *Estudos / Pesquisa:* Sky Cobalt `#0284C7`
  - *Supervisão:* Warm Honey `#D97706`
  - *Administrativo / Faturamento:* Slate Violet `#6366F1`
- **Priority Status:**
  - *Alta:* Crimson Wine `#BE123C` (bg: `#FFE4E6`)
  - *Média:* Amber Gold `#B45309` (bg: `#FEF3C7`)
  - *Baixa:* Deep Slate `#475569` (bg: `#F1F5F9`)

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is chosen across all roles for its geometric purity, open counterforms, and high legibility at micro scales (essential for clinical logs, diagnostic codes, and dosage/session notes).

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Headlines:** Use weights `700` and `600` with subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to provide commanding yet refined orientation at column and module tops.
- **Body:** Set strictly in regular `400` with generous line-heights (`1.6x` for long-form case notes) to safeguard reading comfort during extensive supervision readouts.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** High contrast, uppercase for semantic categories (`label-sm`), medium weight `600` for form headers, badge counts, and context descriptors.

## Layout & Spacing

The system implements an asymmetrical dashboard architecture with an ergonomic persistent sidebar and a 12-column adaptive fluid canvas.

### Layout Distribution
- **Sidebar Column:** Fixed at `260px` on desktop (`>= 1280px`), collapsible to `72px` icon rail on tablet (`768px - 1024px`), and converted into a bottom sheet or off-canvas drawer on mobile (`< 768px`).
- **Workspace Canvas:** Uses fluid distribution with dynamic columns:
  - *Kanban Boards (Tarefas):* 3 equal-width columns (`minmax(280px, 1fr)`) separated by `1.25rem` gutters.
  - *Split Detail Panels (Formulários & Prontuários):* 7-column main task stream alongside a 5-column persistent context/summary sidebar.
- **Paddings & Airflow:**
  - Card internals require `space-md` (`1rem`) on compact views and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on clinical detail cards to keep patient logs readable and uncluttered.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and high-contrast skeuomorphism. It utilizes subtle layered luminescence and precise hairline borders to create clean structural depth.

### Tonal Surface Hierarchy
1. **Base Layer (Level 0):** Background canvas `#F1F5F9`. Matte, glare-free, non-reflective.
2. **Surface Layer (Level 1):** Main operational cards, kanban lanes, and list containers in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline outline in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Interactive (Level 2):** Draggable kanban cards on hover, context popovers, dropdown menus, and hover states on appointment tiles. Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
4. **Overlay Modals & Drawers (Level 3):** Prontuário modals and appointment editors. Overlay backdrop: `#0F172A` at `40%` opacity with a subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-sm`). Card shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(0, 86, 150, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces approachable curvature to instill calm without compromising structural density.

### Radius Distribution
- **Cards & Panes (`rounded-xl` / `1rem`):** Main clinical cards, kanban containers, and summary modules.
- **Inputs & Action Triggers (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` to `0.75rem`):** Form inputs, dropdown selectors, and standard buttons.
- **Pills & Status Tags (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Priority tags, context badges (e.g., *atendimento*, *supervisão*), notification bubbles, and user avatars.

## Components

### 1. Navigation Sidebar
- **Aesthetic:** Dark slate blue background (`#0B192C` or `#003E6B`) with crisp light typography, establishing clear separation from the workspace.
- **Context Dots:** Interactive round tags (`w-2.5 h-2.5`) corresponding to clinical segments (*atendimento*, *estudos*, *supervisão*, *administração*) featuring active hover ring indicators.
- **User Footer:** Compact avatar block with CRP registration label, psychologist name, and a discreet status switch.

### 2. Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#005696`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `0.5rem`. Smooth hover transition to `#023E8A`. Focus ring `2px ring-offset-2 ring-[#005696]`.
- **Secondary / Action Ghost:** Border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#334155`, background `#FFFFFF`. Hover to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Specialized "Concluir / Salvar":** Dual styling option utilizing `#0F766E` (tertiary emerald) or `#005696` with affirmative icons.

### 3. Inputs & Forms
- **Fields:** Pure white surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.625rem 0.875rem`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts dynamically to `#005696` with a soft ambient shadow ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 86, 150, 0.15)`).
- **Segmented Controls (Ex: Tarefa / Compromisso):** Contained pill track in `#E2E8F0` with an active slider tab in `#0F172A` or `#005696` and white text.

### 4. Kanban & Task Cards
- **Lane Containers:** Soft slate `#E2E8F0` tinted with subtle blue-gray, `rounded-xl`, `p-3`, column counters in pill badges.
- **Task Tiles:** Crisp white `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, `p-3.5`, `rounded-lg`. Left accent strip or top dot indicating category context.
- **Sub-elements:** Title in `body-md` (bold `600`), meta row holding due date with calendar icon and priority badge.

### 5. Badges & Chips
- **Priority Indicators:** Compact capsules (`py-0.5 px-2`, text `label-sm`).
  - *Alta:* Tinted carmine badge with deep red text.
  - *Média:* Tinted amber badge with deep ochre text.
  - *Baixa:* Tinted slate with dark neutral text.
- **Context Tags:** Semi-transparent soft tone with crisp matching dot: `flex items-center gap-1.5`.

### 6. Clinical Timeline & Supervision Logs
- Continuous 1.5px vertical guide line in `#CBD5E1` connecting session events.
- Nodes styled as solid circles reflecting session type with timestamp stamps set in `body-sm` `#64748B`.
